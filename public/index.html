Our story

From a small window. A tight menu. A lot of heart

Favo started with a simple idea — that great coffee should be accessible to everyone. We are a small cafe based in Reyno Ridge, eMalahleni, and we show up at every service ready to make your cup the best part of your morning.

Every drink is dialled in by the same small team. iXchangers making the coffee — with care, consistency, and the kind of welcome that turns first-timers into regulars. We keep things tight on purpose: a focused menu, a short window each day.
